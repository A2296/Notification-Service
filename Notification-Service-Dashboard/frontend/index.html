How NotifyFlow works

What the service does, how to start sending and where to get help.

ABOUT

What is NotifyFlow?

NotifyFlow sends email, SMS and in-app notifications for your business through one API. Your server makes one request; NotifyFlow checks it, delivers it through the right provider, retries temporary failures and records every status change, so you can see what happened to each message.

  • ✉ EmailThrough any SMTP email provider
  • ◌ SMSThrough Twilio, with delivery reports
  • ▣ In-appKept in each user's inbox in your app, with read tracking

GET STARTED

Send your first notification

  1. Create your business account. Choose Sign in, then Create an account.
  2. Try it from the dashboard. Write a message on Send notification and follow it in Activity.
  3. Generate an API key. The secret is shown once: keep it on your server, never in browser or mobile code.
  4. Send from your server. Call POST /api/v1/notifications with the X-API-Key and X-API-Secret headers.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why didn't my email or SMS arrive?

Open Settings → Delivery channels. A channel marked logged only is not connected to a provider yet, so its messages are written to the server log instead of being sent. The public demo runs this way. If a notification shows failed in Activity, the provider's error is shown beside it: fix the cause, then click Retry.

What do the statuses mean?
  • pending Waiting to be sent, or scheduled for later
  • processing Being handed to the provider
  • sent Accepted by the email or SMS provider
  • delivered Confirmed: in the user's inbox (in-app) or reported delivered by Twilio (SMS)
  • failed Could not be delivered, even after retries; the reason is shown in Activity
Can I see whether people read my notifications?

For in-app notifications, yes. When your app marks a message as read (PATCH /api/v1/notifications/{id}/read), it counts toward the In-app read rate on the Overview. Email and SMS opens are not tracked: open tracking is unreliable, because many email apps open or block images automatically, and it would mean changing the messages you send.

How do I avoid sending the same message twice?

Send an Idempotency-Key header, such as order-10482-shipped. If the same request is repeated with the same key (for example after a network timeout), NotifyFlow returns the original notification instead of sending it again.

What happens if I send too many requests?

Requests over your limit get HTTP 429 Too many requests. Wait a minute and try again. Your limits are listed under Settings → Your limits.

What happens when I reach my monthly limit?

New notifications are refused with HTTP 429 and the code MONTHLY_LIMIT_REACHED until the limit resets on the 1st of next month. Notifications already accepted are still delivered, and repeating a request with the same Idempotency-Key still returns the original. Recurring schedules skip their runs and show why. Your usage is on the Overview and in Settings → Plan and usage; to raise the limit, ask for a bigger plan.

I lost my API secret. Can I see it again?

No. The secret is stored only as a one-way hash, so it cannot be shown again. Generate a new key on API credentials, update your server, then revoke the old key.

I lost the phone with my authenticator app.

Sign in with one of the recovery codes you saved when you turned on two-factor authentication, in place of the 6-digit code. Each code works once. Then, in Settings, turn two-factor off (using another recovery code) and on again to link your new phone.
